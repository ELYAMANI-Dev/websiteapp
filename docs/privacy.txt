Privacy Policy for OTMATRANS

Effective date: November 15, 2026

OTMATRANS is provided by OTMATRANS. This policy explains how the mobile app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app allows users to save operational records, task notes, work progress, project follow-up entries, and local business preferences. This information is stored on the device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
OTMATRANS does not require account registration. It does not upload operation records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share user records or preferences with OTMATRANS or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity and operations tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operation records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.
