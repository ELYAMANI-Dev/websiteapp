App name
CHRONO COMPANY

Short description
Manage operations, projects, and daily business workflows from one place.

Full description
CHRONO COMPANY helps founders, managers, and service teams track operational work with clarity and speed. The app combines daily task management, operational record tracking, progress monitoring, and a business-ready dashboard in one focused mobile workflow.

Create local records, assign responsibility, monitor due work, update progress, and review performance from a simple, modern overview. Records stay on the device so teams can stay organized without creating account friction or relying on cloud sync.
